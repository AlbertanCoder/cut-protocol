# W4-3 — KNIFE-EDGES

**Definition.** A knife-edge is a place where a *defensible alternative* choice of denominator,
ruler, cut, or tree moves a headline by **more than the A5 minimum detectable effect (3.5 pt on
n ≈ 537 for a churning treatment)**. Below-MDE items are listed separately at the end because
several of them are load-bearing for *interpretation* even when they cannot move a level.

All magnitudes re-derived here from the recorded artifacts. `MEASURED` unless marked.

---

## TIER 2 — below MDE for the level, but load-bearing for interpretation

### K12. `judged` (537) vs canonical (553) — **2.23–2.67 pt level, up to +22.72 pt of headroom**
The level move is below MDE. **The exposure is not.** Because `A1/rig/schema.mjs:83` drops
zero-slot days, refusing every out-of-band satisfiable day scores **100% on 537** and **+0.00 on
553**. W3-7's entire headline table is on 537. Its membership was verified invariant across all 24
dumps (`empty = 16` everywhere), so nothing was gamed — but the denominator is a scoreboard the
player controls and the delta is **2.98% inflated** (+14.96 published vs **+14.53** canonical).
**The single highest-value item in this file.**

### K13. Partial refusal — invisible to *both* safe denominators
24 of the oracle's 163 rescues (14.7%) pass by shipping **fewer meals**. `slotsFilled > 0` holds, so
`judged` keeps them and `planned` keeps them. In-band days carrying an unfilled slot: 4.36% →
7.01%. **No denominator defends against this; only a filled-slot co-report does.** LNS is clean
(0/50); W3-7's combined arm was never checked.

### K14. Rig vs route pool shape — 0.32 pt level, **but day-level pairing is invalid**
80 of 640 records differ in content (K2c); discordant b=7 c=9, \|b−c\|=2 vs an A5 floor of 7.84.
Comparable for a level, **never paired day-by-day**. W3-4 (rig) and W3-7 (route) both published
"+12.60" for the same lever; the honest comparison is **+12.97 vs +12.60**, and it only exists
because both also published a 537 line.

### K15. Label-cut vs proof-cut for "satisfiable" — **0.69 pt** [MEASURED]
`415/553 = 75.05%` (tier label) vs `435/585 = 74.36%` (only the 18 personas with an actual
infeasibility proof). The label discards 20 in-band and 66 failing days and they nearly cancel.
**A genuinely reassuring negative result** — the weakest link in the canonical denominator turns
out not to be a knife-edge. Report it, because "IMPOSSIBLE is a label, not a proof" otherwise reads
as an unquantified threat.

### K16. W3-1's "48.3 days/seed above AMDR" — **70% of it is keto** [MEASURED]
Re-derived: 145 pooled = 48.3/seed, of which **102 (34.0/seed) are keto days**, where high fat %E

**exempts keto**, so the defect its fix addresses is **14.3 days/seed = 3.5% of passes**, not 48.3

Measuring against *achieved* rather than *target* kcal gives 43.7/seed — a third value.

### K17. Pinning as a diagnostic — the number and the arm
95.7% is a **campaign treatment arm** (D10:448, 473/494), not a population. This tree's baseline
measures **77.4% of passing days touch a bound** (campaign baseline: 74.1%). The sharp form:
`P(fail | pinned) = 26.7%` vs a base rate of `22.7%`, while `P(fail | not pinned) = 4.7%`.
**Absence of pinning is informative; presence is not.**

## THE STANDING RULE THIS FILE ARGUES FOR

Every compliance number in this corpus should be published as a **five-tuple**, never as a scalar:

```
rate  ·  denominator (n and its membership rule)  ·  ruler  ·  pool shape (route|rig)  ·  tree (git SHA + dirty?)
```

Four of those five have already produced a >3.5-pt disagreement inside this single run, and the
fifth (tree) produced a 12% verdict churn that nobody had measured until now.

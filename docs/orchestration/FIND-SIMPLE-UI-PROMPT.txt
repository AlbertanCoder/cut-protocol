WHAT I AM LOOKING FOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At some point I had code for a SIMPLIFIED version of the Cut Protocol UI. I
cannot find it. Find it, and if it genuinely does not exist, rebuild it.

What it is:
  - Everything the current app already does, but toned down for an ordinary
    person who is not a numbers person.
  - Similar colours to what we have now, but gentler. Softer. Friendlier.
  - Nothing fancy, nothing clever, nothing intimidating.
  - The reference point is EAT THIS MUCH: you say a few things about yourself,
    it shows you what to eat today, and that is the whole experience. Big
    obvious actions. Very little jargon. No dashboard of statistics.

What it is NOT:
  - It is not a new design system. The tokens and components stay.
  - It is not a rewrite of any logic. See the HARD RULES below.
  - It is not the AURORA RINGLIGHT direction (dark, green-tinted, aurora
    ambience, oversized numerals). That one is documented in CLAUDE.md and is
    superseded.

PHASE 3 - REBUILD, ONLY IF NOTHING IS FOUND AND ONLY AFTER I APPROVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Build it as a SEPARATE, SWITCHABLE surface. Do not replace the existing screens.
The current UI must keep working exactly as it does today.

Target experience, in order of what the user sees:
  1. Sign in.
  2. A handful of plain questions - roughly: are you male or female, how old,
     how tall, what do you weigh, what would you like to weigh, anything you
     cannot eat. One question per screen, big text, big buttons.
  3. "Here is what to eat today." A day of food. Real portions. That is the
     home screen, not a dashboard.
  4. One button to swap a meal you do not like.
  5. One button to say "I ate this."
  6. A weight box, and a simple line showing it going down over time.

Everything else - the ten BMR formulas, the walk-forward numbers, the macro
bands, the Engine tab, the solver diagnostics - stays in the app but moves
behind a "show me the details" affordance. Present, never in the way.

Tone: calm and plain. No jargon on the default path. Never say "TDEE",
"macros", "adherence" or "protocol" on a first-run screen. Say what things
mean in ordinary words.

**Meal 2: Cast-Iron Sirloin, Rice & Peppers** — 529 kcal · 43 g protein
- 130 g Sirloin steak, cooked, lean
- 100 g White rice, cooked
- 300 g Bell peppers
- 5 g Butter

1. Pat the sirloin dry and season heavy with salt and pepper.
2. Get a cast iron pan smoking hot, sear 3-4 min per side for medium-rare.
3. Rest 5 minutes, then slice against the grain.
4. Toss sliced peppers in the same pan 2-3 min until blistered.
5. Plate over rice, melt the butter over the steak.

**Meal 3: Orange-Ginger Chicken (Clean Version)** — 471 kcal · 43 g protein
- 150 g Chicken thigh, cooked, skinless

- 55 g White rice, cooked
- 80 g Broccoli, raw

1. Brown the chicken pieces.
2. Simmer orange juice, coconut aminos and ginger to a glaze; thicken with the cornstarch slurry.
3. Toss chicken through; serve with rice and steamed broccoli.

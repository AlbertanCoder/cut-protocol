## The five mismatches, each verified against the app

| frozen field | frozen value | what the app accepts | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sex` | `'male'` | `"M"` or `"F"` only | `routes/profile.js` `["M","F"].includes` |
| `occupationKey` | `'carpenter'` | not a key. Nearest: **`carpenter-finish`** (1.45, "Carpenter — finish / cabinets") | `OCCUPATION_BY_KEY`, probed: `'carpenter'` → NO |
| `trainingStyle` | `'hypertrophy'` | not a key. Valid: **`weights` \| `mixed` \| `sport` \| `cardio`** | `TRAINING_BY_KEY`, probed: `'hypertrophy'` → NO |
| `weightKg` | `88` | not a profile field at all. `PROFILE_FIELDS` has `startWeightKg` / `goalWeightKg` | `routes/profile.js` `PROFILE_FIELDS` |
| `allergies` | `['gluten','soy']` | not a profile field. The exclusion list is **`excludedFoods`** | `PROFILE_FIELDS`; validator message at `f.excludedFoods` |

`age`, `heightCm`, `minutesPerSession`, `rateLbPerWeek`, `dietaryStyle` and
`unitPref` are all fine.

`hypertrophy` *does* exist — in `training/templates.js`, the workout generator's
vocabulary. The profile's `trainingStyle` is a different, MET-based list in
`activityData.js`. Two vocabularies, same word, different modules. That is very
likely how the mismatch was authored without anyone noticing.

## Why I did not just translate it

The profile is Tier A. Its own comment says it lives in the frozen instrument
*"so the profile cannot be softened after seeing a disappointing result"* —
and I have now seen a disappointing result. A builder who edits the forcing
profile at exactly this moment is the thing that comment exists to prevent,
even when the edit is honest. Especially then.

Note also what is at stake in the translation: `allergies: ['gluten','soy']` is
the load-bearing clause. It is what makes the library thin enough that scaling
alone cannot land the day — the whole forcing function. Moving it to
`excludedFoods` must preserve that, and whether `excludedFoods` drives
`dietaryFilter` the same way `allergies` was assumed to is a question I have
NOT verified and will not guess at.

## What I recommend, for the architect to rule

**Amend the frozen clause to the app's vocabulary, preserving semantics exactly,
and re-freeze at the new bytes.** The minimum faithful translation:

    sex:            'male'          ->  'M'
    occupationKey:  'carpenter'     ->  'carpenter-finish'
    trainingStyle:  'hypertrophy'   ->  'weights'
    weightKg: 88                    ->  startWeightKg: 88
    allergies: ['gluten','soy']     ->  excludedFoods: ['gluten','soy']

Every value keeps its meaning: same trade, same multiplier band, same resistance
training, same mass, same two walls. Nothing is softened — the rate stays
1.0 lb/wk, the walls stay celiac+soy, the style stays non-keto omnivore.

**Before ratifying, one thing needs checking that I have not done:** whether
`excludedFoods` actually enforces the same exclusion `allergies` was assumed to.
If it does not, the forcing function is weaker than the instrument claims, and
that is a finding about the witness's design rather than its plumbing.

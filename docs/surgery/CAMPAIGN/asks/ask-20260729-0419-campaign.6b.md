## REJECTED versus DROPPED — the mechanism behind the whole finding

The builder separated two failure modes the error message had blurred, and this
is the most valuable thing in the exchange:

- **REJECTED** — the validator names it. `sex`, `occupationKey`,
  `trainingStyle`, `dietaryStyle`.
- **DROPPED** — not in `PROFILE_FIELDS`, so never rejected, **silently
  discarded**. `weightKg`, `allergies`.

**A request carrying `allergies` does not fail. It SUCCEEDS, and the value
evaporates.** No error, ever, for anyone to notice.

That is precisely how a forcing profile can look correct indefinitely while
forcing nothing — and it is a better explanation of the original defect than
either of us had reached. Silent-drop is worse than rejection, because
rejection at least produces a witness.

## The builder's finding, verified against the app

`PUT /api/profile` rejects five of the eleven fields. From
`backend/src/routes/profile.js` and `backend/src/lib/activityData.js`:

| field | witness sends | app requires |
|---|---|---|
| `sex` | `'male'` | `"M"` or `"F"` |
| `occupationKey` | `'carpenter'` | a key in `OCCUPATION_BY_KEY`; nearest is `carpenter-finish` |
| `trainingStyle` | `'hypertrophy'` | `weights` \| `mixed` \| `sport` \| `cardio` |
| `weightKg` | `88` | field is `startWeightKg` |
| `allergies` | `['gluten','soy']` | field is `excludedFoods` |

The builder also explained how this was authored without anyone noticing:
`'hypertrophy'` **does exist** — in the training generator's vocabulary. The
profile's `trainingStyle` is a different, MET-based list in `activityData.js`.
Two vocabularies, same word, different meaning.

## The translation strengthens the instrument rather than softening it

`excludedFoods` is the real mechanism: `dietaryFilter` filters the pool through
`excludedByList` → `foodMatchesExclusionTerm` → `exclusionEvidence`, which
unions name/keyword matching with persisted `fdcCategory`, `allergenTags` and
`mayContain` evidence. And both terms are real, deeply-developed families in
`allergenTaxonomy.js`:

- **`gluten`** — label *"Gluten (wheat, barley, rye)"*, synonyms including
  `wheat`, `celiac`, `coeliac`, `gliadin`, `wheat protein`.
- **`soy`** — synonyms including `soya`, `soybean`, `soy sauce`, `bean curd`,
  `black bean sauce`, `worcestershire`, plus soy `fdcCategories` and `offTags`.

So `excludedFoods: ['gluten','soy']` would build genuine walls with synonym
expansion and tag evidence. **This is not softening a profile after a
disappointing result — which is precisely what its comment forbids. It is the
first time the profile would bite at all.**

There is also no prior measurement to invalidate: the witness has never
completed a run, so no result becomes incomparable.

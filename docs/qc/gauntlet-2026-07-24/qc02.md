## Ranked findings

**1 (P1, real leak — the only FAIL basis).** `filterRecipePool` reads ingredient
rows + title + `"Add'l ingredients:"` prose, but NOT free-form step text. Allergens
cooked in via steps only reach the plate. Confirmed load-bearing (allergen absent
from structured rows because the recipe survived the exclusion):
- dairy: *Beef Banh Mi* "stir in 1 TBSP butter"; *Beef Mandi*/*Chicken Mandi* "Heat ghee/oil".
- egg: *Hot and Sour Soup* "add 1 egg… to the soup"; *Lao Naem Khao* "Beat two eggs… dip the balls".
- fish/shellfish: *Sushi* "smoked salmon", "tuna", "half a prawn".
Counts (recipes w/ step leak): dairy 25, gluten 28, redmeat 13, nightshades 13,
fish 6, egg 7, nuts 5, shellfish 1. Many are optional "serve with bread/potatoes"
sides; the above are not. **Not a Stage-1 regression** — identical for the plain
`dairy`/`eggs` checkbox (QC01 scope). Root cause: importer left ingredients in prose.

**2 (PASS, no defect).** Ingredient-row leaks = **0** and title leaks = **0** for
dairy/gluten/nuts/shellfish/soy/redmeat/nightshades/fish/egg. (6 "egg" hits were
`"Egg Plants"`=aubergine in Baba Ghanoush/Sichuan Eggplant — my scanner's false
positive; proves the app's `stripEggPlant`/false-friend guard works.)

**3 (minor).** `pescatarian` is not in `DIETARY_STYLES`; the route 422s it, so the
"self-contradiction" combo is unreachable in-product. Forced into the filter it is a
silent no-op (unknown style → no exclusion); only the `fish` term governs. Defense-in-
depth gap: the filter itself doesn't reject unknown styles. Not user-reachable.

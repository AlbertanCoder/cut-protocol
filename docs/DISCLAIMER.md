# Cut Protocol — Health & Legal Disclaimer

_Last updated: 2026-07-24_

Cut Protocol is a personal nutrition and body-composition **planning tool**. Read
this before you rely on any number it shows you.

## Not medical advice

Cut Protocol is **not a medical device** and does **not** provide medical,
nutritional, or professional healthcare advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The
calorie targets, macronutrient splits, meal plans, micronutrient estimates,
expenditure (TDEE) estimates, and projections it produces are **general
informational estimates generated by formulas and a deterministic solver** — not
a prescription and not personalized clinical guidance.

Nothing in this app is a substitute for the advice of a physician, registered
dietitian, or other qualified health professional. **Always consult a qualified
professional** before starting any diet, calorie deficit, supplement, or exercise
program — and especially before doing so if you:

- are pregnant, breastfeeding, or trying to conceive;
- are under 18;
- have or have had an eating disorder or disordered-eating patterns;
- have diabetes, heart, kidney, liver, thyroid, or any chronic condition;
- take medication whose dosing depends on food intake or body weight;
- have a food allergy or intolerance (see the allergy note below).

## Allergies and dietary restrictions

Cut Protocol filters meal plans against the allergies and exclusions you enter,
and it is designed to **over-exclude rather than under-exclude**. Even so, it
relies on the accuracy and completeness of its underlying food and recipe data,
which can contain errors or omissions. **It cannot guarantee that any plan, food,
or recipe is free of a given allergen.** You are responsible for reading actual
product labels and confirming ingredients before you eat anything. If you have a
severe or life-threatening allergy, do not rely on this app as your only
safeguard.

## Calorie floors and safe rates

The app enforces a hard lower bound on calorie targets and warns on aggressive
rates of loss. These safeguards are conservative defaults, **not** a statement
that any particular intake is safe for you specifically. Very low calorie intakes
can be dangerous and should only be undertaken under professional supervision.

## Eating-disorder note

Calorie tracking and weight-loss tools can worsen disordered eating for some
people. Cut Protocol includes an optional, non-diagnostic self-check and links to
support resources (see the in-app **Wellbeing check**). That check is a screening
prompt only — **it is not a diagnosis.** If you are struggling with food, eating,
or body image, please reach out to a professional or one of the resources listed
in the app.

Cut Protocol — logo candidate technical sheet

Every .svg in this folder, rendered by the browser at 16 / 32 / 64 / 256 px on the app's warm cream (#FBF7F1) and on the app's dark background (oklch(0.15 0.008 255) = #090B0F). Colour master on top row of each pair, -mono master below it. Nothing here is a design judgement — the numbers are geometry measured off the shipped path data at 3072×3072 and downsampled to true 16/24/32 px. The -mono files use #23201D, which sits at 1.22:1 on carbon and is therefore invisible there by design; the mono column on the dark ground is expected to be blank and is shown only to make that explicit.

stem = thinnest parallel-sided ink limb (viewBox units; 16 u = 1 px at 16 px render) gap = narrowest void bounded by two ink faces ctr = largest disc that fits the enclosed counter

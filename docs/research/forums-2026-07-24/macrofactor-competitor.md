# MacroFactor — Competitor Study for Cut Protocol

**Date:** 2026-07-24 · **Analyst:** competitive-research agent

**Source note:** Direct App Store review scrape (justuseapp) returned HTTP 403, and Reddit
site-search kept redirecting to Cronometer forums. Findings below draw on review aggregators
and a secondary summary of r/MacroFactor sentiment — flagged where sentiment is paraphrased
rather than a verbatim quote. No numbers or reviews invented.

## 1. What users LOVE

- **Adaptive TDEE that feels "honest."** The algorithm derives real maintenance calories from
  logged intake vs. weight trend instead of a static formula. Reddit users describe it as
  "honest — it does not pretend to know your metabolism better than your own data," and say it
  "calms anxieties about short-term plateaus." (nutrola summary; calorie-trackers.com)
- **Science credibility.** Built by Stronger By Science (Greg Nuckols, Eric Trexler); trusted by
  intermediate lifters. (nutrola; feastgood)
- **No ads, verified food DB, fast logging.** "Fastest food logger on the market," ~50% fewer
  taps than MyFitnessPal; entries human-reviewed before adding. (feastgood; intakenutrition)
- **Cheaper than MFP Premium** ($71.99/yr vs $79.99/yr). (macrofactor.com vs MFP 2025)

## 2. What users WISH FOR / complain about (ranked)

1. **No free tier** — paywall after 7–14 day trial is the #1 hesitation. (feastgood; fitnesstoolsreviewed)
2. **No AI photo logging** — most-cited "missing vs newer competitors." (nutrola)
3. **No meal planning / recipe suggestions** — "it only tracks what you log"; no recipe
   discovery, users can only save their own. (fitnesstoolsreviewed; feastgood)
4. **No desktop/web version.** (feastgood)
5. **Steep learning curve** — "over-engineered for casual users." (fitnesstoolsreviewed)
6. **Limited micronutrients** vs Cronometer. (fitnesstoolsreviewed)
7. **Requires consistent daily weigh-ins** or the edge degrades. (fitnesstoolsreviewed)
8. Smaller food DB than MFP's ~20M entries; weak non-English support. (intakenutrition; nutrola)

## 3. Cut Protocol vs MacroFactor

**AHEAD:**
- **Deterministic meal solver** — MacroFactor's #3 complaint. MF tracks; CP *builds* compliant
  plans to target. This is the moat.
- **Offline & private** — MF is cloud/subscription; CP is local.
- **One-time / owned vs subscription** — kills complaints #1.
- **Zero-tolerance allergy/diet filtering, cost/time/taste filters, grocery lists** — MF has none.
- **Desktop app exists** (Electron) — MF's #4 gap.

**BEHIND:**
- Logging UX polish & speed (MF = gold standard, 50% fewer taps).
- Barcode DB + verified food-database size (MF human-reviewed; MFP huge).
- No mobile (MF is mobile-first; CP is desktop).
- No photo/AI food logging.
- Onboarding polish — CP also risks MF's "learning curve" trap.

## 4. Single most valuable thing to borrow

**MacroFactor's adaptive-TDEE trust UX** — not the math (CP already learns expenditure), but how
MF *presents* it: a visible expenditure trend line, plain-language "why your target changed"
explanations, and framing plateaus as normal. CP's algorithm is invisible; making the learning
legible would convert its strongest hidden feature into its most compelling one — while the meal
solver remains the thing MacroFactor structurally cannot answer.

# Competitive Research: Cronometer, Lose It!, Carbon Diet Coach

_Compiled 2026-07-24. Sources are public reviews/forums/comparisons; no reviews invented. Reddit's own threads were not directly reachable via search (US-only), so sentiment is drawn from Cronometer's official forums, app-store aggregations, and 2026 comparison writeups that quote Reddit._

## 1. Cronometer — micronutrient depth
Cronometer is the undisputed micro king: **84 nutrients** (13 vitamins, 17 minerals, amino-acid and fatty-acid profiles) from a ~1.1M-food database sourced from USDA FoodData Central + NCCDB, **no user-submitted values** in the verified set. Reviewers call it "the gold standard for clinical nutrition data integrity" and "the most thorough tool without a dietitian writing a spreadsheet by hand." Yes, people want this — it's the reason users tolerate a thinner coaching layer.
**Cut Protocol comparison:** CP's per-ingredient-gram rollup produces the same class of micro visibility, but *automatically from a built plan* rather than from manual logging. That's arguably better UX for the micro-conscious user — Cronometer makes you log to see gaps; CP builds a plan that already closes them. CP is behind on breadth of the food table and lab-verified provenance.

## 2. Carbon / Lose It / MacroFactor — adaptive coaching & habits
- **Carbon (Layne Norton):** 4.8★ / 5,500+ App Store reviews. Loved for **weekly check-ins** that adjust macros on adherence + weight trend ("feedback loop that functions as accountability") and structured **reverse-diet / phase** management. Carbon holds targets until you prove you hit them.
- **MacroFactor:** widely rated the **stronger adaptive-TDEE algorithm** — expenditure from what you actually eat + weight trend, accurate after 2–3 weeks, transparent charts.
- **Lose It!:** valued for a 63M-item DB, barcode "Scan It," and AI photo logging ("Snap It").
Habit features users value across all: reminders/streaks, quick barcode + photo logging, curated food DBs.

## 3. Ranked feature requests (across all three)
1. **Adaptive TDEE** — the #1 unmet Cronometer request; users are leaving for MacroFactor/Carbon over it.
2. **Push-notification reminders / streaks** — top Carbon complaint ("needs push notification reminders").
3. **Meal planning / meal building** — *none of the three build a compliant plan*; Carbon users ask for recipes/meal planning; restaurant/home-cooked mapping is weak everywhere.
4. **Better/curated + international food DB** — Cronometer UK/EU gaps; Lose It accuracy "inconsistent."
5. **Less paywall / fairer price** — Lose It moved barcode, macros, meal plans behind Premium; Carbon has no free tier ("shouldn't have to pay").
6. **Different macros per day / flexible targets.**

## 4. Where Cut Protocol is ahead vs behind
**Uniquely ahead:**
- **Deterministic meal solver** — none of them *build* the compliant plan; they track/coach. This is CP's moat.
- **Auto micro rollup** from real per-ingredient grams (Cronometer depth without manual logging).
- **Adaptive TDEE** — closes Cronometer's most-requested gap.
- **Allergy filtering, any-horizon plans, cost/time filters.**
- **Offline + private, one-time purchase** vs Carbon $8–12/mo, Lose It escalating paywall.

**Behind:**
- **Food DB size/provenance** — Cronometer 1.1M USDA-verified, Lose It 63M.
- **No barcode scanner / AI photo logging.**
- **No mobile app** (desktop-only) — all three are mobile-first.
- **No habit/streak/reminder coaching or push notifications** — the retention hooks Carbon/Lose It lean on.

# Wishlist & Willingness-to-Pay — Nutrition/Fitness App Market Sentiment (2026-07-24)

Market-research sweep for Cut Protocol (desktop, offline, private, one-time-purchase, meal solver). Honest read. Reddit blocks direct fetch; findings drawn from web-indexed sentiment, forum threads, and app-market data.

## 1. Do people want DESKTOP / OFFLINE / PRIVATE / one-time-purchase — or niche?
**Real but a minority.** Mass-market spend is overwhelmingly subscription: ~96.5% of consumer spending on health & fitness apps comes from mobile subscriptions ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1483573/app-spending-subscriptions-and-one-time-purchase-by-category)). Against that, a vocal, growing privacy/anti-sub niche exists and is well-served by open tools: [OpenNutriTracker](https://github.com/simonoppowa/OpenNutriTracker) (open source, local AES-encrypted, self-hostable), [Privacy Friendly Food Tracker](https://secuso.aifb.kit.edu/english/894.php) (no data collection), Dietify (fully offline, no accounts), OmNom Notes (no login), QuietVitality (offline, single-file browser). Whole guides now rank "lifetime purchase" nutrition apps ([Nutrient Metrics](https://www.nutrientmetrics.com/en/guides/best-lifetime-purchase-nutrition-apps-2026)). **Desktop specifically is the weakest leg** — even Cronometer power users who "use the website for the majority of my use" ([forum](https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/3859/application-on-phone-vs-website-on-computer)) still keep the phone for on-the-go/barcode logging. Desktop-*only* fights the dominant logging behavior.

## 2. Subscription fatigue
**Strong and loud, not yet dominant in wallets.** MyFitnessPal paywalling the barcode scanner ($79.99–$99.99/yr) triggered mass migration to Cronometer and spawned apps explicitly marketed "for people tired of expensive subscriptions" (OptimalYou). "App fatigue" and "my favorite apps are ditching one-time payments" are recurring complaints ([Tom's Guide thread — title only, 403 on fetch](https://forums.tomsguide.com/threads/most-of-my-favorite-apps-are-ditching-one-time-payments-for-subscriptions-%E2%80%94-this-is-a-problem.531893/)). Sentiment is genuine; conversion to paid one-time is a smaller slice than the noise suggests.

## 3. Most-requested "no app does this well"
- **Accurate, curated food database** — #1 gripe. Crowdsourced DBs are "riddled with errors" (same chicken breast: 165 vs 284 cal) ([Fitia](https://fitia.app/learn/article/best-calorie-counter-apps-rd-criteria-2026)).
- **Auto meal-planning that actually hits macros** with foods you like, budget, and restrictions ([Eat This Much](https://www.eatthismuch.com/meal-planner/) is the reference people still want bettered) — this is Cut Protocol's solver. Validated demand.
- Custom foods/goals **not** behind a paywall ([MyNetDiary](https://www.mynetdiary.com/myfitnesspal-alternatives.html)).
- Adaptive TDEE coaching (MacroFactor's differentiator).

## 4. Week-1 dealbreakers
- **Logging tedium** → the top abandonment cause.
- **Wrong database numbers** → distrust before week's end.
- **Forced login / paywalled basics** (custom foods, barcode).
- Log becomes a guilt "log of excesses" with no actionable guidance.

## 5. Blunt verdict
Desktop/offline/one-time is a **strength for a defensible niche, a liability for mass audience.** The niche — privacy-conscious, subscription-fatigued, data-owning power users who do serious cutting/recomp at a desk (i.e., Shad's own profile) — is real, underserved, and will pay once for a deterministic solver + local data. The mass dieter logs on a phone, scans barcodes, and subscribes. **Realistic target user:** the quantified-self / anti-subscription serious cutter. Position as "own-your-data, pay-once, real solver," NOT a MyFitnessPal killer. **Two honest liabilities to solve:** (a) desktop-only kills on-the-go/barcode logging — the dominant behavior; consider a companion mobile-log or import path. (b) One-time revenue can't fund ongoing food-DB maintenance — the exact decay that causes the #1 dealbreaker. That's the core business tension to answer.
